The community — four streets in the heart of the village

Quinn Farm sits in the heart of Greely Village, on the Greely Loop — the village’s walking and cycling circuit — which puts it closer to the village core than most of Greely’s estate communities. The community is carried on four streets: Still Meadow Way, the longest street with 68 homes in the City’s address records, Rangeland Avenue with 26, Perennial Way with 19 and Springrun Place with 7 — 120 homes in all. The street names tell the design story: meadows, rangeland and perennial plantings — an eco-community planned around the rural character of the village it sits in.

Because lots are still releasing, Quinn Farm offers two different buys in one community: a new home built on a newly released lot, or a resale on one of the finished streets where the landscaping is already in. Either way, the setting is the same — a village-centre address, on the Loop, minutes on foot or by bike from the village’s daily services.

Living in Quinn Farm

Daily life here is the village-centre end of Greely: four quiet streets on the Greely Loop, with the village core about 1.6 km north — The Shoppes of Greely plaza on Bank Street for groceries and everyday services, the Greely Community Centre and the library branch on Meadow Drive. Bank Street (Ottawa Road 31) is the spine north: downtown Ottawa is roughly half an hour in good traffic. The honest transit picture matches the rest of Greely: no regular OC Transpo route runs through the village — the Thursday Shopper Route 304 is the one exception — so Quinn Farm is a driver’s address, and school buses, run by the Ottawa Student Transportation Authority, are the transit that matters to families here.

Schools in Quinn Farm

Quinn Farm’s designated school paths are confirmed in the four boards’ own school locators in November 2026, for the 2026-2027 school year — the Ottawa-Carleton District School Board (public, English and French Immersion programs), the Ottawa Catholic School Board, the Conseil des écoles publiques de l’Est de l’Ontario (French public) and the Conseil des écoles catholiques du Centre-Est (French Catholic). The locators returned the same path for addresses on both sides of the community. One clear line per path:

Public, English program (OCDSB): Greely Elementary School (Junior Kindergarten to Grade 6) → Metcalfe Public School (Grades 7 to 8) → Osgoode Township High School (Grades 9 to 12).

Public, Early French Immersion (OCDSB): Castor Valley Elementary School (Junior Kindergarten to Grade 8) → Osgoode Township High School (Grades 9 to 12).

Catholic (OCSB): St. Mary School (Junior Kindergarten to Grade 6) → St. Mark Intermediate (Grades 7 to 8) → St. Mark High School (Grades 9 to 12).

French public (CEPEO): École élémentaire publique Michel-Dupuis → École secondaire publique Pierre-de-Blois (Grades 7 to 12).

French Catholic (CECCE): École élémentaire catholique Jonathan-Pitre → École secondaire catholique Pierre-Savard.
