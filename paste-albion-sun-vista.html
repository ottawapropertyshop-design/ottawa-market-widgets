Buying a land-lease home in Albion Sun Vista, Greely — what is different

Start with the tenure, because everything else follows from it. In a Greely freehold purchase you buy the home and the land under it. Here you buy the home — a real, MLS®-listed property that you own — and you lease the site from Parkbridge under a land-lease agreement, for a monthly fee. Listing copy in these communities typically shows that fee covering the land lease together with property taxes and water and sewer services, which is why the carrying costs here read differently from a freehold home’s: no separate tax bill, no well or septic to maintain. In exchange, the purchase price of the home itself is well below what a comparable freehold bungalow costs in Greely — that gap is the whole proposition, and it is why these two communities have a loyal following.
