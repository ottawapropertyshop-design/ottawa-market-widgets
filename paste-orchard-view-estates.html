The community — twelve streets around the ponds

The community is carried on twelve streets in two pockets. Living in Orchard View Estates

Daily life here is the quiet, south end of Greely: estate streets around the ponds, with the village core about 4.1 km north — The Shoppes of Greely plaza on Bank Street for groceries and everyday services, the Greely Community Centre and the library branch on Meadow Drive. The honest transit picture matches the rest of Greely: no regular OC Transpo route runs through the village — the Thursday Shopper Route 304 is the one exception — so Orchard View Estates is a driver’s address, and school buses, run by the Ottawa Student Transportation Authority, are the transit that matters to families here.
