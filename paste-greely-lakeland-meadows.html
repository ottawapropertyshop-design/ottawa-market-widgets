The community — five streets around the lake

Lakeland Meadows is compact and complete: five streets, 113 homes in the City of Ottawa’s address records, in Greely’s southwest. Lakeshore Drive is the spine — 52 of the 113 homes — following the community lake. Cadieux Way and Summerview Terrace loop through the treed lots on the north side, and Scottanne Street and Tommary Drive carry 18 and 14 homes at the community’s north end. The brick entrance sign still carries the name the community was first marketed under — Lakeland Estates — while the subdivision itself is Lakeland Meadows.

Lakeland Meadows Ltd. developed the subdivision and Omega Homes built and sold the homes: the first phase was draft-approved in 2011 with 34 executive lots — an Ontario Municipal Board appeal by the Lakeland Lotowners Association was dismissed in February 2012 — and Omega’s own lot plan shows those lots across Cadieux Way and the Summerview ridge, at.81 to 1.62 acres, many with lake views. The built community runs on private wells and septic systems, the standard Greely country-estate servicing. Omega lists Lakeland Meadows as sold out, so there are no new lots releasing here: the community is finished, and its market is resale.

A second phase has been through the City’s planning process on the lands north of the built community, fronting Old Prescott Road — planned, in the City’s file, to connect to the communal servicing system that serves Shadow Ridge. Those lands are not built and not selling today, and this guide covers the built community only; when a future phase releases, it will get its own guide.

Living in Lakeland Meadows

Daily life here is the quiet end of Greely: five streets, big treed lots, the lake at the community’s heart, and no through traffic. The built community runs on private wells and septic systems. The village core is about 2.9 km northeast — The Shoppes of Greely plaza on Bank Street for groceries and everyday services, the Greely Community Centre and the library branch on Meadow Drive. Bank Street (Ottawa Road 31) is the spine north: downtown Ottawa is roughly half an hour in good traffic. The honest transit picture matches the rest of Greely: no regular OC Transpo route runs through the village — the Thursday Shopper Route 304 is the one exception — so Lakeland Meadows is a driver’s address, and school buses, run by the Ottawa Student Transportation Authority, are the transit that matters to families here.
