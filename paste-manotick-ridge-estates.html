The Manotick Ridge Estates story — how this Manotick estate pocket came to be

Manotick Ridge Estates stands on the high farm ground north of Manotick village, on the west bank of the Rideau River. The village below it grew around the mill on the river; the ridge above stayed in farms long after the village streets filled in — big fields, tree lines and frontage on the two roads that frame the pocket today, Rideau Valley Drive North (the old River Road) on the west and Spratt Road on the east. When the ground finally subdivided, it did not become suburbia. It became an estate pocket, and it was built the slow way: lots first, houses one at a time.

The subdivision of record is two registered plans. The legal descriptions carried on the pocket’s own properties put Caracara Drive, Winnards Perch Way and the lower reach of Black Canary Drive on Registered Plan 4M-1261, and the northern Black Canary lots on Registered Plan 4M-1341 — two phases of one Manotick Ridge Estates program, tied together by the shared easements their deeds still carry. The model was land, not houses: lots of about two acres, sold to buyers who commissioned their own custom homes. That is why no two Manotick Ridge Estates houses match — and why the pocket’s builders are a roll of Ottawa’s custom trade, one home at a time. Canterra Design’s 6,500-square-foot estate home in the pocket is one documented example; the rest of the street is the same method repeated seventy-five times. The build-out ran long — the earliest pocket homes date to 2004–2006, the newest house on Black Canary Drive to 2025, and vacant lots still come to market — so the ridge is a mature estate pocket that is still, quietly, filling in.

Manotick Ridge Estates questions, answered

Where is Manotick Ridge Estates?
Manotick Ridge Estates is an estate pocket in Manotick, Ottawa — on the west bank of the Rideau River between River Road and Spratt Road, about a kilometre north of Manotick village, under Mitch Owens Road. Its four subdivision streets hold 75 homes, and this guide’s ground also carries Knott Crescent (10 homes) and the Old Mill Way loop (30 homes) — 115 homes on 6 streets in the City’s address records. Every pocket address is postal Manotick, K4M 0A1.

Who built the homes in Manotick Ridge Estates?
No single builder did. The pocket was subdivided in two registered plans — Plan 4M-1261 and Plan 4M-1341 — and sold as estate lots; each buyer commissioned a custom home from their own builder and architect. That is why the streets read as a collection, not a model range — Canterra Design’s 6,500-square-foot estate home in the pocket is one documented build among seventy-five one-offs.

How big are the lots in Manotick Ridge Estates?
About two acres, typically. Across the pocket the estate lots run from roughly 1.35 acres up to four and a half — 1.97 and 2.23 acres among the sampled lots — and the pocket’s RR2 (Rural Residential) zoning protects the large-lot pattern. Even the smallest Manotick Ridge Estates lots are far beyond suburban dimensions — space is the pocket’s defining feature.

What services do Manotick Ridge Estates homes run on?
Hydro and natural gas run in the subdivision, and the roads are public City roads with no gate. Every home carries its own drilled well and septic system, installed at the owner’s cost — so budget the homework into any purchase: water potability and flow tests, and a septic inspection.

Which schools serve Manotick Ridge Estates?
For the pocket’s addresses: Manotick Public School (JK–5) → Kars on the Rideau (6–8) → Osgoode Township High School and South Carleton High School, both listed by the board’s locator; St. Leonard (JK–6) → St. Mark (7–12) at 1040 Dozois Road, just east of the pocket; Michel-Dupuis → Pierre-de-Blois in French public; and Jonathan-Pitre → Pierre-Savard by exact-address designation in French Catholic. Confirm any exact address in the board’s locator before you buy.

Is Manotick Ridge Estates part of Manotick village — or of Greely?
Neither, quite. It is postal Manotick and stands on Manotick’s west bank, but it lies outside the Manotick village guide’s boundary — which is why it has its own guide. It is not Greely either: Greely’s addresses are K4P, east across the river and Stagecoach Road. One quirk to know: the listing system codes the pocket’s homes under the Manotick East district (8005), even though the pocket is west-bank — searches for it have to be drawn on the map, the way this guide’s panels are.
