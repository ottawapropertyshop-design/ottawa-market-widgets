Why rent in Knollsbrook

Knollsbrook is finished — and renters who find it tend to love that. There is no construction phase coming, no half-empty new streets, just a settled pocket with a public school inside it, a splash-pad park on Foxfield Drive, and the Fallowfield Station hub at the top of the hill for both the Transitway and the VIA train to Toronto. Families rent here for the established streets and the school; professionals posted to Ottawa rent here because they can step off a train and be home in minutes. The trade-off is supply: because the pocket is small and built out, listings are sporadic — and good ones go fast.

Commuting from a Knollsbrook rental

This is Knollsbrook's superpower. Fallowfield Station at 3347 Fallowfield Road sits immediately north of the pocket: VIA Rail runs the Toronto–Ottawa corridor with roughly 8–10 trains a day in each direction, the OC Transpo Transitway station handles the bus commute, and the Park & Ride holds about 1,709 cars. Frequent routes 74 and 75 anchor the service, with local routes 70, 73, and 173, Connexion routes 275, 277, and 279, and school route 682. Inside the pocket, route 173 loops the interior streets (Foxfield/Knollsbrook, Foxfield/Holitman, Wolfgang/Fallowfield), and the Greenbank/Larkin stop adds routes 70 and 75. By car, Woodroffe Avenue and Highway 416 are minutes away via Fallowfield, and downtown runs roughly 25 minutes off-peak. The City's long-term plan extends O-Train Line 1 to Fallowfield sometime after 2031.

Greenbank Park at 895 Greenbank Road adds a second splash pad at the pocket's west edge. For a small neighbourhood, that is a lot of summer and winter infrastructure within a short walk of every street.

What Ottawa landlords ask for — come prepared

Ontario landlords typically ask for a credit check, proof of income or an employment letter, references from previous landlords, and first and last month's rent as deposit — the only legal deposit in Ontario; "key money" is not permitted. Ontario's Standard Form of Lease is mandatory for most tenancies. In a pocket where listings are sporadic and go fast, have your documents ready before you tour: I can get your application in front of the listing agent the same day.

Ontario renting essentials

Most leases start with a one-year term, then continue month-to-month. Rent control applies to units first occupied before November 15, 2018. "No pets" clauses are void under Ontario's Residential Tenancies Act, which makes freehold houses the pet-friendly choice. Utilities are often tenant-paid; snow removal and lawn care are negotiable.
