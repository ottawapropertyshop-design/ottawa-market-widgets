The neighbourhood: Cedargrove and Fraserdale, Barrhaven

Cedargrove and Fraserdale is the established, premium pocket of Barrhaven's north end — mature Tartan-built streets around Cedarview Road and Jockvale Road, south of Strandherd Drive, with townhouses and newer growth filling in along its southern edge. It is the part of Barrhaven buyers pick when they want finished streets and big detached lots instead of a construction zone, while staying minutes from everything Barrhaven has built since.

Schools, parks and daily life

Families here draw on a deep school bench (confirm your exact catchment on the board locator before buying): Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School (149 Berrigan Drive, Grades 7–12), Cedarview Middle School (2760 Cedarview Road), Berrigan Elementary School (199 Berrigan Drive) and Chapman Mills Public School, with Catholic options including St. Elizabeth Ann Seton School. Shopping and getting around

The pocket itself is purely residential — part of its appeal — with Barrhaven's big corridors a short drive away: Barrhaven Town Centre and the Marketplace at Barrhaven for everyday shopping and dining, Costco Barrhaven at 4315 Strandherd Drive, and the Cineplex Odeon Barrhaven at 131 Riocan Avenue. The Strandherd Transitway station serves the wider area, and the drive downtown runs about 25 to 30 minutes off-peak.
