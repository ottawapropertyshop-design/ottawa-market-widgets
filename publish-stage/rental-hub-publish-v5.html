Commuting from a Barrhaven rental

The Southwest Transitway runs through Barrhaven with stations at Fallowfield, Longfields, Strandherd, Marketplace, Barrhaven Centre, Beatrice, and Nepean Woods — Fallowfield station pairs a large Park & Ride with the VIA Rail station for intercity trips. Highway 416 borders Barrhaven to the west; Strandherd Drive, Greenbank Road, Woodroffe Avenue, and Fallowfield Road are the main arterials. Off-peak drives to downtown run roughly 25–30 minutes. What Ottawa landlords ask for — come prepared

Competition for the best Barrhaven houses for rent is real, and prepared renters win. Ontario landlords typically ask for: a credit check, an employment letter or proof of income, references from previous landlords, and first and last month's rent as deposit (in Ontario, that's the only legal deposit — "key money" or damage deposits are not permitted). Ontario's Standard Form of Lease is mandatory for most residential tenancies. Ontario renting essentials

A few rules every Barrhaven renter should know: most leases start with a one-year term, then continue month-to-month automatically. Rent control applies to units first occupied before November 15, 2018 — newer homes are exempt from the annual guideline cap. "No pets" clauses are void under Ontario's Residential Tenancies Act (a landlord cannot evict you for having a pet), though condo declarations can restrict pets in condo rentals — one more reason freehold house rentals are the pet-friendly choice. In freehold rentals, utilities are often paid by the tenant and snow removal/lawn care is negotiable — always confirm what's included before you sign.
