Schools & Catchments

The school cluster is the headline: Mary Honeywell Elementary School (54 Kennevale Drive, OCDSB JK–6), St. Mother Teresa High School (440 Longfields Drive, OCSB 7–12), Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School (149 Berrigan Drive, OCDSB 7–12), St. Luke Catholic Elementary School (60 Mountshannon Drive), the French-public École secondaire publique Pierre-de-Blois and École Pierre-Elliott-Trudeau, plus the private Ottawa Christian School (255 Tartan Drive, JK–8). confirm your exact catchment in the OCDSB school locator and the equivalent finders on the other three boards’ sites before you sign a lease.

Commute & Transportation

Strandherd Drive and Longfields Drive are the arterials; the Strandherd and Longfields Transitway stations are both within reach, and the off-peak downtown drive runs about 25–30 minutes via the 416 and Queensway.

Parks, Trails & Recreation

Neighbourhood parks, sports fields and splash pads are distributed through the 1990s–2000s streetscapes; the Jock River trail system lies just south.

Shopping, Groceries & Restaurants

The Strandherd Road plazas and the Longfields corridor (including the Farm Boy-anchored plazas) cover daily shopping; Chapman Mills Marketplace is minutes south for the full shop.

Heritage Park Condo Rental Stock & What It Rents Like

The rental stock is 1990s–2000s resale condo townhouses and low-rise condo apartments — newer than Pheasant Run’s 1970s–80s complexes, with the more generous floor plans of that era. Two and three-bedroom townhome layouts dominate; expect garages, in-suite laundry and established condo corporations with mature reserve funds.

Safety & Street Character

A 1990s–2000s family pocket — settled streets, active school zones at bell times, quiet evenings. Standard advice: visit the complex in the evening before applying.

Services & Healthcare

Walk-in clinics, dental practices, pharmacies and banks along the Strandherd and Longfields corridors; hospital care via Queensway Carleton Hospital to the northwest.

Churches & Community

Longfields Community Church and other Barrhaven congregations are nearby; the district-wide list is on the Barrhaven condos-for-rent hub.

Water, Rivers & Landmarks

The Jock River runs south of the pocket — trail access without riverfront pricing.

Entertainment & Leisure

Cineplex Odeon Barrhaven at Chapman Mills Marketplace (with XSCAPE) is minutes south; the Walter Baker Sports Centre is a short drive north.

Daycares & Childcare

Licensed centres serve the family streets here; the City View Centre for Child & Family Services (1099 Longfields Drive) is nearby. Barrhaven-wide waitlists apply — start early.
