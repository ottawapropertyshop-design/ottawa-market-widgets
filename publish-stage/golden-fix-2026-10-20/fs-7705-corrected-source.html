If you want a central Barrhaven address with a built-in conversation starter, start with the On the Green Neighbourhood. This is the only Barrhaven sub-area named after a golf business — the On the Green Driving Range & Mini Golf at 3232 Jockvale Road, a family-run range with an 18-hole mini putt on the pocket's western edge. Tucked between Jockvale Road, Greenbank Road, Strandherd Road and the rail tracks (AMP area 7705), On the Green is 1980s-era Barrhaven at its most practical: established detached and townhome streets, quick transit on Route 70, and the Strandherd retail strip a few minutes south.

What sets On the Green apart is position, not prestige. You are in the middle of everything Barrhaven offers without paying the premium of the river or golf-course pockets. The homes are older and often renovated, the lots are honest 1980s sizes, and daily errands — groceries, the pharmacy, a bucket of balls after work — happen within a few kilometres. If you are browsing Ottawa houses for sale and want a central, well-connected Barrhaven base at a mid-range price point, this On the Green neighbourhood guide is your starting point.

Schools & Catchments

No school building sits inside the 7705 pocket itself, but families here are well served by nearby secondaries: Mother Teresa High School, John McCrae Secondary School and St. Joseph High School are all close. One local feature sheet for an Exeter Drive home counted 11 schools with catchments serving the address across the public and Catholic boards — which is exactly why you should confirm your specific street in the OCDSB, OCSB, CECCE and CEPEO school finders before you buy. Catchments in this part of Barrhaven follow street-level boundaries.

Commute & Transportation

On the Green is one of Barrhaven's better-connected pockets for transit. OC Transpo Route 70 runs right through the neighbourhood on Tartan Drive, Exeter Drive and Wessex Drive, connecting to Fallowfield Station — one Exeter Drive listing advertises a transit stop less than a minute's walk away. The Strandherd Park & Ride (3680 Strandherd Drive, 300+ spaces) sits just south of the pocket for commuters who drive partway, and the Fallowfield VIA Rail station links Barrhaven to the Toronto–Ottawa corridor. By car, Greenbank Road and Strandherd Drive are the main arterials, and downtown Ottawa is typically 25–30 minutes off-peak.

Housing Stock & Price Context

Expect 1980s construction on streets like Wessex, Exeter, Bartley and Benlark: detached two-storeys and townhomes, many renovated — one Benlark Road detached was built in 1982 and is typical of the pocket's stock. Services, Healthcare & Daycare

No hospital or major medical clinic sits inside On the Green; residents use Barrhaven-area clinics, dental offices and pharmacies a short drive away. For childcare, Guide and Grow Preschool operates at 3013 Jockvale Road (at Barrhaven United Church, on the pocket's western edge); no daycare inside the pocket itself has been confirmed yet.

Churches & Community

Sequoia Community Church at 255 Tartan Drive sits inside the pocket (meeting at Ottawa Christian School), while Barrhaven United Church (3013 Jockvale Road) and Barrhaven Fellowship Christian Reformed Church (3058 Jockvale Road) stand on Jockvale Road at the pocket's western edge. No On the Green–specific community association was found — Barrhaven-wide representation applies.

Safety & Street Character

On the Green reads as a settled, middle-Barrhaven family pocket: quiet 1980s streets, kids walking to nearby schools, and steady local traffic around the range on Jockvale Road. With stock from this era, line up a thorough inspection of the big-ticket items — roof, foundation, original furnace and windows — even on renovated homes.

Future Development

On the Green is essentially built out. Change here comes from infill and renovation, not new subdivisions — which suits buyers who want an established, central streetscape rather than a construction zone. (New-build Barrhaven is a different story, told on the Half Moon Bay and Stonebridge pages.)
