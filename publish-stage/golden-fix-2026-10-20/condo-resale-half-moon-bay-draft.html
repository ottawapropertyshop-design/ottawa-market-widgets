Condo fees in Half Moon Bay

New stacked towns reset the fee conversation: Caivan is waiving condo fees for 12 months at the Conservancy — real money, but read it as a first-year incentive, not the long-run fee. New-build fees almost always rise after year one as the corporation finds its real costs, so underwrite the year-two fee, not the waived year. Benchmark Ottawa 2026 norms — roughly $0.50–$0.90 per square foot per month — and compare the builder's first-year budget line by line.
