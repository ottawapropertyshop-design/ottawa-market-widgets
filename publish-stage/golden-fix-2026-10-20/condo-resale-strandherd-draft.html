Commute and transportation from Strandherd

The anchor is Strandherd station on the Southwest Transitway: since the 2012 right-of-way, buses bypass Greenbank traffic, and routes 70, 75, 80, 173, 275, 279, 305 and the N75 night bus serve the station. The Strandherd Park & Ride (3680 Strandherd Drive) sits on the corridor. Local routing: route 173 runs via Cobble Hill Drive and Marivista Drive; route 110 covers Strandherd Drive. Drivers are 25–30 minutes from downtown Ottawa off-peak.
