Schools and catchments near Stonebridge

Nearby schools include Adrienne Clarkson Elementary School St. Cecilia Catholic School John McCrae Secondary Mother Teresa High School. Adrienne Clarkson Elementary (170 Stoneway Drive, JK–6, triple-track English and French Immersion, ~435 students) sits on a large property adjacent to Stonecrest Park — students access soccer pitches, baseball diamonds, play structures and a community rink through a City co-operative arrangement, and Children's Village at Stoneway runs an extended day program in the school. St. Cecilia Catholic School (3490 Cambrian Road) is the area Catholic elementary. Nearby secondaries: John McCrae, Mother Teresa and St. Joseph High School (3333 Greenbank Road). Catchments are street-specific — confirm with the OCDSB school locator and the other boards' finders before buying.

Commute and transportation from Stonebridge

OC Transpo route 75 (frequent) runs along Greenbank Road — Stonebridge's western edge — to Cambrian and the Minto Recreation Complex, and since April 2025 extends south of Kilbirnie Drive via River Mist Road and Cappamore Drive. The Strandherd and Fallowfield Park & Rides are the closest. Drivers are 25–30 minutes from downtown Ottawa off-peak.

Parks, trails and recreation near Stonebridge

Kilbirnie Park and W.C. Levesque Park are the two major parks — large fields and play structures. Stonecrest Park sits adjacent to Adrienne Clarkson Elementary (shared pitches, diamonds, splash pad across the street). The Stonebridge Trail is a 24-acre walking trail along the Jock River with wildlife watching; several parks have splash pads, and the community association readies the Kilbirnie outdoor rink each winter. The Minto Recreation Complex (3500 Cambrian Road) — six-lane pool, two NHL rinks, gym, walking track — is the shared anchor with Half Moon Bay.

Water, rivers and landmarks near Stonebridge

The Jock River forms the community's northern border, and the Stonebridge Trail runs 24 acres along it. The golf course itself is the water-adjacent landmark — and the watch item: the City held a community information session in January 2019 on potential development in the golf community, and course-land redevelopment was a 2018 election issue. A 2019 resident-supported levy aimed to halt residential development on the course lands for ten years — verify current status before treating any fairway view as permanent.

Entertainment and leisure near Stonebridge
