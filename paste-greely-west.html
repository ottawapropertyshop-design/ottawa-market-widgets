Living in Greely West

Daily life here is the quiet, west end of Greely: estate streets around the park, with the village core about 3.7 km northeast — The Shoppes of Greely plaza on Bank Street for groceries and everyday services, the Greely Community Centre and the library branch on Meadow Drive. Stagecoach Road and Manotick Station Road carry you north to Mitch Owens Road, or south into the township; Bank Street (Ottawa Road 31) is the spine north toward the city, and Manotick village is a short drive west across the Rideau River. The honest transit picture matches the rest of Greely: no regular OC Transpo route runs through the village — the Thursday Shopper Route 304 is the one exception — so Greely West is a driver’s address, and school buses, run by the Ottawa Student Transportation Authority, are the transit that matters to families here.
