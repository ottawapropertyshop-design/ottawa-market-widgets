The Forest Lake Estates story — how this pocket came to be

Forest Lake Estates starts with Sunset Lakes Developments, the builder behind most of Greely’s lake and estate communities since 1990 — Sunset Lakes itself, Woodstream, South Village, Water’s Edge, Cedar Lakes and Lakewood Trails all came from the same hand, and Forest Lake Estates is the newest of them. The idea travelled with the builder from the earlier communities: big lots, water at the centre, and the natural landscape left standing instead of cleared flat. Here the landscape is forest — a ten-acre municipal woodlot anchors the lands, and the plan was drawn so the community threads through the trees rather than replacing them.

Photo: Tomasz R. Witek — the community lake, Forest Lake Estates.

The setting is Greely’s Emerald corner. West and north of Forest Lake Estates lies Emerald Links, the Patterson family’s golf course community; directly north, sharing Forest Lake Estates’ boundary, Emerald Estates is the next Sunset Lakes community coming behind it; and immediately south are the established estate streets of Greely West. Fox Valley Road is the way in — south to Apple Orchard Road, or north through the Emerald Links fabric — and the developer’s office sits on Fox Valley Road at the community’s own corner. It is a quiet, wooded edge of the village, minutes from the village core and a straight run up Bank Street to the city.

The amenities are arriving the way the community is — in step with the lots. The lake and ponds are in, with private community access; a pickleball court overlooking the water and gazebos by the shore were added as the current phase filled in. What will not change is the framework the plan locked in: one-acre lots, the conservation easement on the rear half of most lots, and the canopy rule. Forest Lake Estates in ten years will look like Forest Lake Estates now, only finished — that is exactly what the remaining-lots buyer is buying into.
