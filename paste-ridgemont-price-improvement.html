If you are new to Ottawa, here is how schools work here. Four publicly funded school boards serve the city — English public, English Catholic, French public and French Catholic — and a family enrols with ONE of them. It is not one system with a menu of schools to pick from: each board designates its own schools for every address. So a single home has four possible school paths — one per board — and which path your children follow depends on the board you choose.
