Living in Water’s Edge — life on Lake Zont

The community turns around Lake Zont, a spring-fed lake at its heart. The shoreline is sandy and limestone-framed — one home’s listing describes 100 feet of it — with a dock for the residents, tennis and basketball courts, walking trails and a picnic gazebo. The homes are custom executive builds, each one different, on lots of about half an acre; the streets run on private services, a drilled well and a septic system at each home, which is the standard servicing picture for Greely’s estate pockets. There are no phases left to release: the two streets are finished, and the community you see is the community as it will stay.

Photo: Tomasz R. Witek — the Lake Zont shoreline, Water’s Edge.

Daily life here is the quiet, north end of the village: the entrance off Parkway Road puts the village core — the Parkway Road and Bank Street shops and services, the Greely Community Centre and the library branch on Meadow Drive — about 0.7 km away. Is Water’s Edge inside Greely, or is it Metcalfe?
It is Greely.
