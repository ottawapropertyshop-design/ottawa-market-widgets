The School

Collège catholique Mer Bleue is Orléans’ south-end French Catholic secondary school, at 6401 chemin Renaud in Orléans (K1W 0H8), serving grades 7 to 12 under the CECCE. Its own site lists the programs families ask about most:

  • International Baccalaureate. The IB Programme d’éducation intermédiaire (PÉI), including a certification pathway, and the IB Programme du diplôme for the senior years.

  • TechnoDesign. A Concentration TechnoDesign and a Préconcentration TechnoDesign, for students building toward design and technology pathways.

  • MHS Fabrication. A Specialist High Skills Major in Fabrication, plus a programme d’enrichissement.

No feeder list is printed in this guide, because no primary source publishes one for Mer Bleue — and on this board a feeder list would mislead anyway: designation is not inherited from a named elementary school, it is answered for the exact home address. That is the check offered on this page.

Questions Families Ask First

Does buying a home near Mer Bleue guarantee my child a place at the school?
No. School assignment is always the school board’s decision, designations can change, and programs fill. A home close to Mer Bleue is a starting point for a search — never a guarantee. Confirm the schools assigned to the exact address with the board and directly with any other school you are considering, before you firm up an offer.

How do I check which school an Orléans address belongs to?
Send me the exact address — not just the street or the neighbourhood — and I will confirm it with the board and reply the same day with its designated elementary and secondary schools. Designation on this board is decided address by address: two houses on the same street can be designated to different schools, so the street alone never settles it.

Why is there no catchment map on this page?
Because CECCE has never published one. Unlike boards that issue a boundary PDF for every school, CECCE publishes Mer Bleue’s designation only as per-address answers — there is no official line to draw. Any Mer Bleue catchment map you find elsewhere is a third-party approximation. This series does not publish approximations as boundaries, so this guide settles designation the only way this board allows — address by address.

What programs does Mer Bleue offer?
The school’s own site lists its programs: the IB Programme d’éducation intermédiaire (PÉI) and IB Programme du diplôme, the Concentration and Préconcentration TechnoDesign, the MHS (Specialist High Skills Major) in Fabrication, and a programme d’enrichissement. Program availability and entry requirements are the board’s to confirm for your child’s entry year.

What is Mer Bleue’s Fraser rating?
The Fraser Institute’s Report Card on Ontario’s Secondary Schools 2025 (2023/24 data) rates Collège catholique Mer Bleue at 7.6 out of 10, #130 of 747 Ontario secondary schools. A rating is one input — it says nothing about whether a specific home is designated for the school, which is the address check this guide is built around.

Is Mer Bleue an English Catholic (OCSB) school?
No. Mer Bleue is a CECCE school — French-language Catholic. The Ottawa Catholic School Board (OCSB) is the English Catholic board, with its own schools and its own designation rules. An Orléans family choosing between the two boards is choosing a language of instruction first, and an address check second.
