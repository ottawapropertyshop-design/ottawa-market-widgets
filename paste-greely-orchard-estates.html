The Greely Orchard Estates story — a Sunset Lakes community in Greely

Greely Orchard Estates is one of the eight Greely communities of Sunset Lakes Developments, the developer founded in 1990 by Dan Anderson, with partner Marcel Renaud. Anderson built his company on an idea he brought home from waterfront communities in Florida, Arizona and New Zealand: homes grouped around water, on generous lots, with room to breathe. The first Sunset Lakes residents arrived in 1993, and the Orchard lands followed Woodstream and South Village in the developer’s sequence — the fourth of its eight communities, ahead of Water’s Edge, Cedar Lakes, Lakewood Trails and Forest Lake Estates.

The community’s paper trail is a matter of legal record — and it is worth stating exactly what that record does and does not prove. Greely Orchard is registered as Plans 4M-1237, 4M-1238 and 4M-1295, with 1374421 Ontario Ltd. as the owner of record and Sunset Lakes Developments as the contractor; the subdivision’s servicing — roadways, utilities, storm water management, ditches and amenities — was certified substantially performed on June 30, 2009. Blossom Trail Drive is the community’s proven core: all three plans meet on that one street, end to end, and Spartan Grove Street’s low end and one Apple Orchard Road block — 6470, a lot of Plan 4M-1295 — sit on the plans too. Two pieces of the community’s ground rest on the developer’s own maps rather than a public plan record: Byles Way, which Sunset Lakes’ Estates map draws inside the community, and the Manotick Station Road frontage, where the Estates lots fronted the road. No publicly available legal description proves either one’s plan, and this guide says so rather than dressing it up. Families were already living here before the 2009 certificate: 1400 Byles Way, the only home on its street, is a custom home built in 2007 on a two-acre lot. The name fits the ground — Apple Orchard Road runs along the community’s north side, and the Orchard name has been on this corner of Greely ever since.


Photo: Tomasz R. Witek — Blossom Trail Drive, the community’s core street — all three of its registered plans meet on it.

By the time Sunset Lakes Developments came back to Greely Orchard in 2014, there was almost nothing left to sell. The developer’s spring 2014 newsletter offered the final release — Lots 16 and 17, “two peaceful & spacious 2 acre estate lots in one of our most established communities” — and the two lots, marketed that August as beautiful two-acre lots with an on-site pond, were still the only Orchard lots on the developer’s available-lots list in 2015 — and when the list was published again in June 2016, Lots 16 and 17 were still on it, at lower prices. That was the tail end: today Greely Orchard Estates is fully built out, its homes change hands by resale, and the developer’s work has moved on to Forest Lake Estates and the newest Cedar Lakes phases.
