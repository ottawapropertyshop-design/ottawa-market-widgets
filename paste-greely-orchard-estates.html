There is no builder model row here and no two Greely Orchard homes are alike — what you are buying is a particular house, on a particular two-acre piece of Greely, and it has to be judged on its own merits.

The Greely Orchard Estates story — a Sunset Lakes community in Greely

Greely Orchard Estates is one of the eight Greely communities of Sunset Lakes Developments, the developer founded in 1990 by Dan Anderson, with partner Marcel Renaud. Anderson built his company on an idea he brought home from waterfront communities in Florida, Arizona and New Zealand: homes grouped around water, on generous lots, with room to breathe. The first Sunset Lakes residents arrived in 1993, and the Orchard lands followed Woodstream and South Village in the developer’s sequence — the fourth of its eight communities, ahead of Water’s Edge, Cedar Lakes, Lakewood Trails and Forest Lake Estates.

The community’s paper trail is a matter of legal record. Greely Orchard is registered as Plans 4M-1237, 4M-1238 and 4M-1295, with 1374421 Ontario Ltd. as the owner of record and Sunset Lakes Developments as the contractor; the subdivision’s servicing — roadways, utilities, storm water management, ditches and amenities — was certified substantially performed on June 30, 2009. But families were already living here before that certificate: 1400 Byles Way, the only home on its street, is a custom home built in 2007 on a two-acre lot. The name fits the ground the community stands on — Apple Orchard Road runs along its west side, and the Orchard name has been on this corner of Greely ever since.


Photo: Tomasz R. Witek — Blossom Trail Drive, the street that closes Greely Orchard Estates on its east side.

By the time Sunset Lakes Developments came back to Greely Orchard in 2014, there was almost nothing left to sell. The developer’s spring 2014 newsletter offered the final release — Lots 16 and 17, “two peaceful & spacious 2 acre estate lots in one of our most established communities” — and the two lots, marketed that August as beautiful two-acre lots with an on-site pond, were still the only Orchard lots on the developer’s available-lots list in 2015. That was the tail end: today Greely Orchard Estates is fully built out, its homes change hands by resale, and the developer’s work has moved on to Forest Lake Estates and the newest Cedar Lakes phases.
