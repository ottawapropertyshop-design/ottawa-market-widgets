The Short Answer

Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School is an Ottawa-Carleton District School Board (OCDSB) secondary school at 149 Berrigan Drive in Barrhaven (Nepean), teaching Grades 7 to 12 — it opened in 2009 as the OCDSB’s first Grades 7 to 12 school, shares its campus with Berrigan Elementary School at the corner of Longfields Drive and Berrigan Drive, and welcomes approximately 2,250 students a year. The board publishes its secondary boundary as its own entry — the Longfields-Davidson Heights S.S. English and Immersion Grade 9 to 12 Attendance Boundary on its official secondary boundary index, updated January 10, 2025, drawn on the board’s map printed September 2024 — and answers for one exact address at a time through its School Locator, listed as up to date for the 2026–2027 school year. In the Fraser Institute’s 2025 secondary report card, Longfields-Davidson Heights rates 7.0 out of 10, ranked #220 of 747 in Ontario. One boundary covers both secondary programs: English and Immersion students draw from the same line.

The Catchment, From the Board’s Own Line

A catchment — the board calls it an attendance boundary — is the area a school draws its students from, and for Longfields-Davidson Heights three things about the line matter to a buyer. First, the board decides, not the map: the school at the end of the street is not evidence, and proximity is a hint, not an answer — the locator result on the exact address is. Second, this school is two schools in one building: it takes its own students at Grade 7 and keeps them to Grade 12, so a family inside this line is not shopping for a middle school at all — the elementary feeder is the only earlier question. Third, lines move: boards review boundaries as neighbourhoods grow and enrolment shifts — this catchment absorbed the whole Chapman Mills Public School area between 2020 and 2023 for exactly that reason — which is why this series names the board source and its update date on every guide, and re-checks its catchments every September.

The option strip deserves a plain explanation, because listing write-ups blur it. Inside the strip — the Balmoral Drive and Via Campanile corridor on Longfields’ west edge — the board gives the family a choice between two named schools: Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School or John McCrae Secondary School. A home there is not assigned to Longfields-Davidson Heights; it is eligible to choose it, under the board’s option rules, and the choice is the family’s to make and the board’s to administer. That is a genuinely useful position for a buyer who wants the Grades 7 to 12 door open — and it is a different position from a designated address, which is why the listings below are drawn on the designated area only. One distinction is worth stating plainly because buyers mix it up: transportation eligibility through OSTA, which runs home-to-school transportation for the OCDSB, is a distance test applied inside the boundary — it is not the boundary itself.

The Path Inward: Who Feeds Longfields-Davidson Heights

There is no middle-school step. Longfields-Davidson Heights is a Grades 7 to 12 school, so its elementary feeders arrive at Grade 7 and stay to graduation: Berrigan Elementary School (which shares the school’s campus), Adrienne Clarkson Elementary School, Chapman Mills Public School, Farley Mowat Public School, Jockvale Elementary School and Barrhaven Public School. The path in one line: an LDH feeder elementary school (Junior Kindergarten to Grade 6) → Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School (Grades 7 to 12).

The Chapman Mills path is the one families ask about most. Chapman Mills Public School’s catchment used to run to Cedarview Middle School and John McCrae Secondary School; under OCDSB Report 19-125 the board redirected it to Longfields-Davidson Heights for Grades 7 to 12, phased in from September 2020 and complete in 2023. A Chapman Mills P.S. address today is a Longfields-Davidson Heights path — which is why the Chapman Mills streets west of Woodroffe Avenue sit inside this guide’s designated area, while the streets east of Woodroffe (Half Moon Bay Public School’s side) sit inside John McCrae’s.

Questions Families Ask First

Does buying a home inside the Longfields-Davidson Heights catchment guarantee my child a place at the school?
No. School assignment is always the school board’s decision, boundaries can change, and programs fill. A home inside the line is a strong starting point for a search — never a guarantee. Confirm the schools assigned to the exact address with the board’s school locator, and directly with any other school you are considering, before you firm up an offer.

How do I check which school a Barrhaven address belongs to?
Use the Ottawa-Carleton District School Board’s official School Locator, which the board lists as up to date for the 2026–2027 school year. Enter the exact address — not just the street or the neighbourhood — because this boundary runs along lot lines and road corridors: two homes on the same street can sit on different sides of it.

What is the option strip on the Longfields-Davidson Heights map?
One strip on the board’s map is hatched as an option rather than designated — the Balmoral Drive and Via Campanile corridor on Longfields’ west edge. Families there may choose Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School or John McCrae Secondary School. A home in the option strip is eligible to choose Longfields-Davidson Heights under the board’s option rules — it is not assigned there — which is why this guide’s listings are drawn on the designated area only.

Do the English and French Immersion programs at Longfields-Davidson Heights share one boundary?
Yes. The board publishes a single combined entry — Longfields-Davidson Heights S.S. English and Immersion, Grades 9 to 12 — and one boundary map for both programs. The line on this page is that combined line, so it serves an English-program family and an Immersion family the same way.

Which schools feed into Longfields-Davidson Heights?
Its elementary feeders — Berrigan Elementary School, Adrienne Clarkson Elementary School, Chapman Mills Public School, Farley Mowat Public School, Jockvale Elementary School and Barrhaven Public School — arrive at Grade 7, because the school itself teaches Grades 7 to 12. There is no separate middle school on this path.

What does Longfields-Davidson Heights’ Fraser rating of 7.0 actually measure?
It is the Fraser Institute’s report-card rating for the school — 7.0 out of 10, ranked #220 of 747 in the 2025 secondary report card. It is one input among many: it says nothing about whether a specific address falls inside the line, and it is not a promise about any individual child’s experience. This series prints the figure with its source and year so you can weigh it properly.

Are the listings on this page really inside the catchment?
Yes — that is the promise this page is built on. The searches run on the school’s own official board boundary, digitized from the board’s map, not on a postal code or a neighbourhood outline. Every listing address the searches display was checked against that line, and the numbers in each snapshot count exactly the homes its map displays — so when you click a home here, it sits inside Longfields-Davidson Heights’ designated area.
