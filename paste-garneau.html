The Short Answer

École secondaire catholique Garneau is a CECCE French-language Catholic secondary school at 6588 rue Carrière in Orléans, teaching Grades 7 to 12 and welcoming more than 1,250 students. It is not an Ottawa Catholic School Board (OCSB) school — OCSB is English Catholic; Garneau is French Catholic, which is why it never appears in the OCSB locator and why its catchment works differently. In the Fraser Institute’s 2025 secondary report card, Garneau rates 7.9 out of 10, ranked #95 of 747 in Ontario — the highest-rated secondary school in Orléans in that report card. Six CECCE elementary schools feed it. And its catchment is the combined zone of those six feeders — a zone the board has never published as a map, which is why the board’s live locator is embedded in this guide instead of a drawn boundary.

The School and Its Six Feeders

École secondaire catholique Garneau (6588 rue Carrière, Orléans, K1C 1J4) is the Grades 7 to 12 anchor for French Catholic families across Orléans and the Des Pins pocket of Gloucester. There is no middle-school step: its elementary feeders arrive at Grade 7 and stay to graduation. The path in one line: a Garneau feeder elementary school (Maternelle to Grade 6) → École secondaire catholique Garneau (Grades 7 to 12). The six feeders, each verified against CECCE sources on November 15, 2026:

  • École élémentaire catholique des Pins — Maternelle to Grade 6, 1487 promenade Ridgebrook, Gloucester. The one Garneau feeder outside central Orléans: it serves the Ridgebrook area of south Gloucester, so a Garneau search that only looks at Orléans misses this pocket entirely.

  • École élémentaire catholique des Voyageurs — Maternelle to Grade 6, 6030 promenade Voyageur, Orléans. The west Orléans feeder, serving the Convent Glen side of Orléans with more than 390 students.

  • École élémentaire catholique L’Étoile-de-l’Est — Maternelle to Grade 6, 6220 promenade Beauséjour, Orléans. The central Orléans feeder, close to Garneau itself — and the clearest proof on this page that proximity is not designation: homes on its own street can be designated to a different elementary school, which is why the locator below, not the map pin, decides.

  • École élémentaire catholique d’enseignement personnalisé La Source — Maternelle to Grade 6, 1445 promenade Duford, Orléans. La Source is a personalized-learning school, and its admission may work differently from a standard catchment designation — confirm eligibility with CECCE directly before relying on a La Source path for any address.

  • École élémentaire catholique Reine-des-Bois — Maternelle to Grade 6, 1450 promenade Duford, Orléans. The north Orléans feeder, serving the Duford corridor a short walk from La Source.

  • École élémentaire catholique Saint-Joseph d’Orléans — Maternelle to Grade 6, 6664 rue Carrière, Orléans. The feeder beside Garneau itself, on the same campus area of rue Carrière — the board’s locator returns Saint-Joseph d’Orléans and Garneau together for the school’s own address.

What the combined zone means: if a home’s designated elementary school is one of these six, it will generally feed Garneau for Grades 7 to 12 — but “generally” is not a promise. Boundaries and designations change, and La Source may follow different admission rules, so the check that counts is always the locator result on the exact address, for the correct grade and school year.

Questions Families Ask First

Does buying a home near Garneau guarantee my child a place at the school?
No. School assignment is always the school board’s decision, designations can change, and programs fill. A home close to Garneau is a starting point for a search — never a guarantee. Confirm the schools assigned to the exact address with the board’s school locator — embedded in this guide — and directly with any other school you are considering, before you firm up an offer.

How do I check which school an Orléans address belongs to?
Use the CECCE’s own locator, embedded live in this guide. Enter the exact address — not just the street or the neighbourhood — because designation on this board is decided address by address: two houses on the same street can be designated to different schools. The tool returns the designated elementary and secondary schools for that address.

Why is there no catchment map on this page?
Because CECCE has never published one. Unlike boards that issue a boundary PDF for every school, CECCE publishes Garneau’s designation only as per-address answers inside its interactive locator — there is no official line to draw. Any Garneau catchment map you find elsewhere is a third-party approximation. This series does not publish approximations as boundaries, so this guide embeds the board’s live tool instead.

Are the listings on this page inside the Garneau catchment?

Which schools feed into Garneau?
Six CECCE elementary schools, all Maternelle to Grade 6: Des Pins in Gloucester, des Voyageurs, L’Étoile-de-l’Est, La Source, Reine-des-Bois and Saint-Joseph d’Orléans. Their students arrive at Garneau at Grade 7 and stay to Grade 12 — there is no separate middle school on this path.

What is different about La Source, the personalized-learning feeder?
La Source is a personalized-learning elementary school, and its admission may work differently from a standard catchment designation. It is listed among Garneau’s feeders, but before relying on a La Source path for any address, confirm program eligibility with CECCE directly.

What does Garneau’s Fraser rating of 7.9 actually measure?
It is the Fraser Institute’s report-card rating for the school — 7.9 out of 10, ranked #95 of 747 in the 2025 secondary report card, the strongest rating of any Orléans secondary school in that report card. It is one input among many: it says nothing about whether a specific address is designated for Garneau, and it is not a promise about any individual child’s experience.
