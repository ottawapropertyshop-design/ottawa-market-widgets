What Emerald Estates is

Emerald Estates is the marketing name for the Emerald Subdivision, a proposed community of 73 single-detached homes on private wells and septic systems, on lots of roughly 0.4 hectares or more. The owner and applicant of record is 9287043 Canada Corporation — Sunset Lakes Developments, the Greely developer behind Sunset Lakes, Forest Lake Estates, and several other village communities. The lands sit in Part of Lots 3 and 4, Concession 3, in the former Township of Osgoode, and the City file carries the site address as 6544 Jack Pine Crescent, Greely, in Ward 20 – Osgoode.

The draft plan on file — drawn by J.D. Barnes Limited and dated February 1, 2022 — shows the community registering in two phases, 40 lots and then 33 lots. A woodlot enclave owned by the City of Ottawa sits inside the plan’s outline and is not part of the subdivision; the gold perimeter on the map above wraps around it.

Where it sits in Greely

Emerald Estates sits in the northwest of the Village of Greely. Emerald Links Golf & Country Club and its estate lots are to the north, the Emerald Links Phase 3 fabric — Green Links Way and Green Jacket Crescent — is to the west, Fox Valley Road and the Greely West lots are to the south, and the Jack Pine Crescent and White Oak Drive streets are to the east and southeast. The lands sit roughly 500 metres west of Stagecoach Road, well north of Apple Orchard Road; the parcel itself does not front Stagecoach Road, Apple Orchard Road, or Manotick Station Road.

Planning status — what is approved, and what is not

This is the section that matters, stated plainly:

  • Zoning: approved. The companion zoning by-law amendment (City file D02-02-21-0071), which rezoned the lands for the proposed community, was approved by Council on January 26, 2026.

  • Subdivision: not registered. The plan of subdivision (City file D07-16-21-0023) has not been registered. Registration is the step that creates legal lots — without it, there are no lots to sell.

  • No streets, no lot release. No internal streets exist on the ground, and the developer has made no lot release for Emerald Estates. Anyone offering you an Emerald Estates lot today is getting ahead of the planning record.

  • The draft plan can still change. The perimeter on this page is the February 1, 2022 draft plan. An unregistered draft plan can change before registration, so treat the lot count and the boundary as the proposal on file, not a finished survey of lots for sale.

An early transportation study for the project once forecast full build-out by 2027. That was a 2021 planning assumption, not a promise — the zoning decision only arrived in January 2026, and no registration or release has followed it in the records checked for this guide. No honest timeline for lots can be given yet.

Three “Emerald” names — do not confuse them

  • Emerald Estates (this guide) is Sunset Lakes Developments’ planned community beside the golf course — planning stage, nothing selling.

  • Emerald Links is the Patterson family’s golf course, opened in 1990 and now 27 holes, and the built Emerald Links Estates homes around it — a different developer and a finished, lived-in community.

  • Forest Lake Estates, directly south of the Emerald Estates lands, is also a Sunset Lakes Developments community — but it is registered (Plan 4M-1782) and its Phase 1 lots are selling through the developer. Emerald Estates has not reached that stage.

Schools — an honest note

There are no homes and no addresses in Emerald Estates yet, so no school can be designated for it today, and this guide does not guess one. School designation in Ottawa is by exact address, decided by each board. When lots are registered and addresses exist, the designated paths for those addresses can be checked in the boards’ own school locators — the same check every Greely guide in this series is built on. Until then, any claim about which schools an Emerald Estates home would attend would be invented, and you should not rely on one from any source.

Questions buyers ask about Emerald Estates

Can I buy a lot or a home in Emerald Estates now?
No. The subdivision is not registered, no lots have been released, and there are no homes. What exists today is an approved zoning change and a draft plan on file.

Who is the developer?
Sunset Lakes Developments, through its company 9287043 Canada Corporation — the Greely developer behind Sunset Lakes, Forest Lake Estates, Woodstream, South Village, and other village communities.

How many homes are planned?
73 single-detached homes on the draft plan, in two phases of 40 and 33 lots, on private wells and septic systems with lots of roughly 0.4 hectares or more. The draft plan is unregistered and can still change.

Is Emerald Estates part of Emerald Links?
No. Emerald Links is the Patterson family’s golf course and estate community. Emerald Estates is a separate Sunset Lakes Developments project on the lands beside it — the shared “Emerald” word reflects the location, not shared ownership.

When will lots be released?
No release date exists in the planning record, and none is promised here. Registration has to come first. If you want to be told when that changes, call or text me at 613-314-1199 and I will keep you posted as the file moves.
