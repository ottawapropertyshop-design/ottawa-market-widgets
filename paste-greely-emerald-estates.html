What Emerald Estates in Greely is

Emerald Estates is the marketing name for the Emerald Subdivision, a proposed Greely community of 73 single-detached homes on private wells and septic systems, on lots of roughly 0.4 hectares or more. The owner and applicant of record is 9287043 Canada Corporation — Sunset Lakes Developments, the Greely developer behind Sunset Lakes, Forest Lake Estates, and several other Greely village communities. The lands sit in Part of Lots 3 and 4, Concession 3, in the former Township of Osgoode, and the City file carries the site address as 6544 Jack Pine Crescent, Greely, in Ward 20 – Osgoode.

The draft plan on file — drawn by J.D. Barnes Limited and dated February 1, 2022 — shows the Greely community registering in two phases, 40 lots and then 33 lots. A woodlot enclave owned by the City of Ottawa sits inside the plan’s outline and is not part of the Greely subdivision; the gold perimeter on the map above wraps around it.

Where it sits in Greely

Emerald Estates sits in the northwest of the Village of Greely. Emerald Links Golf & Country Club and its Greely estate lots are to the north, the Emerald Links Phase 3 fabric — Green Links Way and Green Jacket Crescent — is to the west, Fox Valley Road and the Greely West lots are to the south, and the Jack Pine Crescent and White Oak Drive streets — both Greely streets — are to the east and southeast. The lands sit roughly 500 metres west of Greely’s Stagecoach Road, well north of Apple Orchard Road; the Greely parcel itself does not front Stagecoach Road, Apple Orchard Road, or Manotick Station Road.

Emerald Estates Greely planning status — what is approved, and what is not

This is the section that matters for Greely buyers, stated plainly:

  • Zoning: approved. The companion zoning by-law amendment (City file D02-02-21-0071), which rezoned the lands for the proposed Greely community, was approved by Council on January 26, 2026.

  • Subdivision: not registered. The plan of subdivision (City file D07-16-21-0023) has not been registered. Registration is the step that creates legal Greely lots — without it, there are no lots to sell.

  • No streets, no lot release. No internal streets exist on the ground, and the developer has made no lot release for Emerald Estates in Greely. Anyone offering you an Emerald Estates lot today is getting ahead of the planning record.

  • The draft plan can still change. The perimeter on this page is the February 1, 2022 draft plan. An unregistered draft plan can change before registration, so treat the lot count and the boundary as the Greely proposal on file, not a finished survey of lots for sale.

An early transportation study for the Greely project once forecast full build-out by 2027. That was a 2021 planning assumption, not a promise — the Greely zoning decision only arrived in January 2026, and no registration or release has followed it in the Greely records checked for this guide. No honest timeline for Greely lots can be given yet.

Three Greely “Emerald” names — do not confuse them

  • Emerald Estates (this guide) is Sunset Lakes Developments’ planned Greely community beside the Greely golf course — planning stage, nothing selling.

  • Emerald Links is the Patterson family’s Greely golf course, opened in 1990 and now 27 holes, and the built Emerald Links Estates homes around it — a different developer and a finished, lived-in Greely community.

  • Forest Lake Estates, directly south of the Emerald Estates lands, is also a Sunset Lakes Developments Greely community — but it is registered (Plan 4M-1782) and its Phase 1 Greely lots are selling through the developer. Emerald Estates in Greely has not reached that stage.

Schools in Emerald Estates, Greely — an honest note

There are no homes and no addresses in Emerald Estates in Greely yet, so no school can be designated for it today, and this guide does not guess one. School designation in Ottawa is by exact address, decided by each board. When lots are registered and addresses exist, the designated paths for those addresses can be checked in the boards’ own school locators — the same check every Greely guide in this series is built on. Until then, any claim about which schools a future Emerald Estates, Greely home would attend would be invented, and you should not rely on one from any source. Greely families who buy in a built Greely community today get their schools designated by exact address — Emerald Estates families will get exactly the same treatment once Greely addresses exist here.

Emerald Estates Greely questions, answered

Can I buy a lot or a home in Emerald Estates, Greely now?
No. The subdivision is not registered, no lots have been released, and there are no Greely homes to buy. What exists today is an approved zoning change and a draft plan on file with the City for this Greely land.

Who is the developer?
Sunset Lakes Developments, through its company 9287043 Canada Corporation — the Greely developer behind Sunset Lakes, Forest Lake Estates, Woodstream, South Village, and other Greely village communities — all of them Greely builds.

How many homes are planned?
73 single-detached Greely homes on the draft plan, in two phases of 40 and 33 Greely lots, on private wells and septic systems with lots of roughly 0.4 hectares or more. The draft plan is unregistered and can still change.

Is Emerald Estates part of Emerald Links?
No. Emerald Links is the Patterson family’s golf course and estate community. Emerald Estates is a separate Sunset Lakes Developments Greely project on the Greely lands beside it — the shared “Emerald” word reflects the location, not shared ownership.

When will lots be released?
No release date exists in the Greely planning record, and none is promised here. Registration has to come first. If you want to be told when that changes for Greely, call or text me at 613-314-1199 and I will keep you posted as the file moves.
