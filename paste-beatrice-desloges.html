The School

École secondaire catholique Béatrice-Desloges is east Orléans’ French Catholic secondary school, at 1999 avenue Provence in Orléans (K4A 3Y6), serving grades 7 to 12 under the CECCE. The school is named for Béatrice Desloges, the Franco-Ontarian teacher who defied Regulation 17’s ban on French-language teaching. What the school’s and board’s own pages verify:

  • Académie des arts. An Arts-études program the board describes as the only francophone Arts-études program offering one arts course per day — music, drama, visual and digital arts, and dance — open to students from grade 7 to grade 12.

  • Specialist High Skills Majors (MHS). The school’s own program pages document its MHS streams, including Construction and Sports; the Ontario SHSM program list for 2025–26 also lists Arts and Culture, Health and Wellness, Hospitality and Tourism, Information and Communications Technology, and Justice, Community Safety and Emergency Services for this school.

  • ÉcoÉcoles. The school holds provincial ÉcoÉcoles certification, per its own site.

No feeder list is printed in this guide, because no primary source publishes one for Béatrice-Desloges — and on this board a feeder list would mislead anyway: designation is not inherited from a named elementary school, it is answered for the exact home address.
